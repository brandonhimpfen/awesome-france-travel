## Official & Planning

Essential government entry portals, tourism boards, and heritage sites across France.

- [Explore France (France.fr)](https://www.france.fr/en/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring regional itineraries, cultural guides, and sustainable travel tips.
- [France Diplomatie - Visas](https://france-visas.gouv.fr/) - Official official visa portal for entry requirements, Schengen regulations, and short-stay applications.
- [Centre des Monuments Nationaux](https://www.monuments-nationaux.fr/en) - Official administration managing over 100 historic monuments, châteaux, and national landmarks across France.

## Transportation

Navigating high-speed rail lines, international Eurostar connections, domestic flights, and regional transport.

- [SNCF Connect](https://www.sncf-connect.com/en-en) - Official national booking platform for TGV INOUI, OUIGO, Intercités, and regional TER train tickets.
- [Eurostar](https://www.eurostar.com/) - High-speed international rail network connecting Paris and Lille with London, Brussels, Amsterdam, and Cologne.
- [Île-de-France Mobilités (RATP)](https://www.iledefrance-mobilites.fr/) - Official transit guide for managing metro, RER, bus, and tram networks in Paris and surrounding regions.
- [Air France](https://www.airfrance.com/) - National flag carrier operating extensive domestic routes and international long-haul flights into major hubs like Paris-Charles de Gaulle (CDG).

## Destinations & Regions

### Paris & Île-de-France

- [Office du Tourisme de Paris](https://www.parisinfo.com/) - Official visitor portal for the capital detailing museum passes, exhibitions, neighborhood guides, and dining.
- [Château de Versailles Official](https://en.chateauversailles.fr/) - Official visitor portal for booking timed-entry passes to the palace, gardens, and Trianon estate.

### Provence & French Riviera (Côte d'Azur)

- [Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur Tourism](https://www.provence-alpes-cotedazur.com/) - Regional travel hub covering lavender fields, the Verdon Gorge, and coastal Mediterranean towns.
- [Nice Côte d'Azur Tourism](https://www.explorenicecotedazur.com/) - Official guide for exploring Nice, Promenade des Anglais, and nearby Riviera villages.

### Bordeaux & Nouvelle-Aquitaine

- [Bordeaux Tourism & Conventions](https://www.bordeaux-tourism.com/) - Official visitor guide for Bordeaux wine heritage, the Cité du Vin, and historic port architecture.
- [Biarrritz Basque Country Tourism](https://www.tourisme-biarrritz.pyrenees-atlantiques.fr/) - Regional guide for surfing coasts, Basque gastronomy, and Pyrenean foothill towns.

### Normandy & Brittany

- [Normandy Tourism Board](https://en.normandie-tourisme.fr/) - Official travel planner for Mont Saint-Michel, D-Day landing beaches, and impressionist coastal towns (Étretat, Honfleur).
- [Brittany Tourism (Tourisme Bretagne)](https://www.brittanytourism.com/) - Regional visitor guide for medieval walled cities, coastal paths (GR 34), and megalithic sites.

### Alps & Mountain Regions

- [Mont Blanc Natural Resort](https://www.montblancnaturalresort.com/) - Official visitor portal for Chamonix Valley alpine lifts, hiking trails, and mountaineering guides.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers, freelancers, and remote tech professionals in France.

- [Welcome to France (Business France)](https://www.welcometofrance.com/) - Official government portal providing guidance on talent passports, remote worker visas, and tax regulations.
- [Station F](https://stationf.co/) - World's largest startup campus based in Paris, offering incubator programs, networking, and coworking infrastructure.

## Safety & Practical Etiquette

Practical advice on emergency contacts, tipping customs, and health services.

- [112 European Emergency Number](https://www.interieur.gouv.fr/) - Direct single European emergency hotline for police, fire, and medical assistance.
- [France IntervieSecours (SOS Urgences)](https://www.interieur.gouv.fr/) - Official safety guidelines and emergency medical services directory.
